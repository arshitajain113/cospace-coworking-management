How it works

CoSpace replaces spreadsheets and paper records with one connected workflow.

Booking steps

  1. Register or log in
    • Choose your role: member, staff or admin
    • Complete your profile
  2. Pick a space
    • Search by name or filter by type and price
    • Check availability for your date
  3. Submit the booking form
  4. Receive a confirmation and track the status

Who can do what

Member
Books spaces, edits or cancels their own bookings and views their dashboard.
Staff
Reviews booking requests and updates their status.
Admin
Adds, edits and removes spaces, and views reports for the whole centre.

Coworking terms

Hot desk means any free desk in the shared area, used on a first-come basis.

Dedicated desk means a desk reserved for one member for the whole month.

See the spaces section for prices and capacity.

Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel a booking?

Open the bookings table and press Cancel next to your booking. You can cancel any booking that is still pending or confirmed.

How long does confirmation take?

Staff review requests during opening hours. You will see a notification as soon as the status changes from pending to confirmed.

Which ID proof can I upload?

Upload a clear JPG, PNG or PDF copy of a government-issued ID.

Is Wi-Fi included?

Yes. Wi-Fi is free in every space, and tea or coffee is included with hot desks.
